Zurk Maps uses the original red and blue 48x64 animation strips, repacked into 512x512 TGA atlases for WoW texture compatibility. No generative edits were used.

Zurk Maps fixed31 visibility pass: blank source frames were omitted from the runtime atlas, the original visible spark pixels were enlarged without redrawing their shape, and a soft faction-colored halo was added so the CC BY source remains readable at the AB capturebar scale.


Additional derivative work for longer in-bar arc animation:
- The local AB_CracklingElectricity_Alliance/Horde TGA atlases were further rebuilt
  from the sourced electricity frames into longer 4x4 (15-frame used) crackling-arc
  atlases so the in-bar 5-base arc can be revealed across the capture bar without
  heavily distorting the original small source frames.
